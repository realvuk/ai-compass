# Project description, about 150 words

Arguments about artificial intelligence are usually drawn as one line, from optimists to doomers. AI Compass starts from a different idea: AI isn't one debate, it's eight. People who agree about how risky advanced AI is often disagree about who should control it, how fast to build it, or who should profit.

The app is a free interactive quiz. You rate 16 statements for a quick, provisional result or 48 for the full one. It shows your position on all eight dimensions, names the closest of eleven illustrative reference camps, and lets you compare with a friend to see where you agree and where you differ.

It runs entirely in the browser, with no account and no tracking. It was created by Vuk Dinić, a computational sociologist, inspired by Kristin Houser's Big Think article on the perspectives dividing the AI world. It is an exploratory educational tool, not a scientifically validated test.
