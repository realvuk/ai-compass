AI isn't one debate. It's eight.

Most conversations about AI put people on a single line, from optimist to doomer. I don't think that line describes how people actually disagree. Someone can be worried about risk and still want AI to be open. Someone else can be relaxed about risk and still want strict rules about who profits.

So I built AI Compass, a short interactive quiz that asks about eight questions separately: risk, pace, access, war, labor, compute, architecture and wealth.

What it does:
• A quick route (16 statements, about 3 minutes) or a full one (48)
• Your position on each of the eight dimensions, with the answers behind it
• A comparison with a friend: where you agree most, where you differ most, and a question to talk over
• No account and no tracking. Your answers stay in your browser

A caveat I care about as a social scientist: this is an exploratory educational tool, not a validated test. The statements and the reference camps are my reading of the debate, and I would like to hear where they are wrong.

The idea grew out of Kristin Houser's Big Think piece "10 perspectives that divide the AI world".
